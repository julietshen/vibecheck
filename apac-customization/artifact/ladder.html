Open safety models for APAC: where the gaps are

Five open models, one harm per language, tested across Southeast Asian languages, Chinese, and romanized Bengali — on one laptop plus two rented GPUs.

Every open safety model holds up on well-resourced languages and falls off a cliff on romanized, low-resource ones. The model marketed as “multilingual” is the one that collapses hardest. And fine-tuning — the fix everyone reaches for — isn’t guaranteed to work.

What the data actually says

Three findings — one of them revised my own hypothesis

1

The gap is the language, not the model type

I guessed steerable models couldn’t read Asian languages. Wrong: cope-b is 100% steerable AND the best multilingual all-rounder. The robust failure is romanized, low-resource Bengali — where every model drops.

2

“Multilingual” is not uniform

Shieldstral — the model sold as multilingual — is best on SEA (1.00) and Chinese (0.95) yet worst on romanized Bengali (0.39). Code-mixed romanized script is out of its distribution. Test the exact languages you serve.

3

Fine-tuning is not magic

LoRA fixed the objective harm (moneylending SEA 0.86→1.00, Chinese 0.77→0.88) but made Bengali offensive worse (0.64→0.31): 270 examples taught a small model to under-flag. Subjective harm × hardest language needs data, not a quick adapter.

The takeaway for a T&S team. No single open model is steerable + multilingual + auditable + cheap — you compose them, and you measure on your own languages. The romanized / low-resource cliff falls hardest on exactly the languages under-represented in the tooling’s training — which is why APAC practitioners contributing regional eval sets and labels to the RMC is infrastructure, not charity.

What ran, and the fine print

Models

  • Shieldstral-3B, Qwen3-1.7B (+LoRA) — local, MLX, Apple-silicon
  • cope-b-a4b, gpt-oss-safeguard-20b — Modal H100
  • Base to own & adapt: Qwen (SEA-Guard / Qwen3Guard build on it). Kimi / MiniMax are rent-by-prompt only.

Data

  • SEA moneylending — synthetic, native-speaker validation needed
  • Chinese — ChiFraud (CC BY-NC 4.0)
  • Bengali — TB-OLID / HASOC (AGPL-3.0), romanized/code-mixed

Method

  • F1 under one generic policy; steerability = release under a permissive policy
  • LoRA: rank 16, 8 layers, ~300 examples
  • All numbers reproduce from the repo’s runners
